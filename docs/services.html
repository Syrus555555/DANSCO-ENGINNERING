Shoring & Formwork Engineering

We engineer shoring systems, concrete formwork, and temporary structures — and provide pre-pour inspections — that keep your construction operations safe, code-compliant, and on schedule, from residential pours to high-rise core walls.

Improperly designed shoring and formwork is one of the leading causes of construction accidents and delays. Our licensed engineers provide complete design packages that satisfy OSHA requirements, local building department requirements, and the structural demands of your specific pour sequence and loading conditions.

In addition to design, we perform pre-pour shoring and formwork inspections to verify the system is built per the engineered drawings before concrete placement — a critical safety and quality checkpoint.

  • Wall, column, beam & foundation form design
  • Shoring system design & load calcs
  • Reshoring & backshoring analysis
  • Elevated slab shoring layouts
  • Pre-pour shoring & formwork inspection
  • Access scaffold design
  • Temporary structure design
  • OSHA-compliant documentation
  • Shop drawing review & approval
  • Field consultation & RFI response

What You Receive

  • Stamped shoring / formwork design drawings
  • Load calculations and material specifications
  • Pour sequence recommendations
  • OSHA submittal-ready documentation package

Structural Inspections

Our licensed engineers perform thorough on-site structural condition assessments and deliver stamped, written reports suitable for permitting, insurance claims, property transactions, milestone recertification, and litigation — typically within 48 hours of the site visit.

A structural inspection from a licensed P.E. carries weight with building departments, insurance carriers, lenders, and courts. We inspect all types of residential, commercial, and industrial structures — documenting existing conditions, identifying deficiencies, and recommending corrective action when needed.

Florida-based projects can rely on Dansco for Milestone (40-year) and Phase 1 / Phase 2 recertifications, threshold inspections, wind mitigation surveys, and post-storm damage documentation. We're equally at home with pre-purchase due diligence and forensic investigations.

  • Structural condition assessments
  • Milestone & 40-year recertification (FL)
  • Phase 1 & Phase 2 structural inspections
  • Threshold inspector services
  • Wind mitigation surveys
  • Pre-purchase due diligence inspections
  • Post-storm & post-fire damage documentation
  • Load capacity evaluations
  • Foundation & slab inspections
  • Concrete deterioration assessments
  • Roof structure inspections
  • Retaining wall evaluations
  • Special inspections per IBC 1704
  • Insurance claim documentation
  • Forensic investigations
  • Expert witness & litigation support
